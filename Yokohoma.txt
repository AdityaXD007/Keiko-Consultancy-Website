Course Structure
Basic Japanese Language Course: Beginner-level classes focusing on fundamental language skills, including Hiragana, Katakana, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation and basic communication strategies.
Advanced Japanese Language Course: Advanced-level classes targeting fluency and proficiency in Japanese, with an emphasis on specialized vocabulary, Kanji, complex grammar structures and cultural values.
Exam Preparation Course: Intensive courses designed to prepare students for exams like JLPT, NAT-TEST, J-TEST, JLCT, J-Cert, Top-J, Skill Test-JFT Basic, etc. at various levels with mock tests, practice exercises and targeted skill-building sessions.
Interview Skills Workshop: Practical workshops providing guidance on interview etiquette, communication techniques, cultural sensitivity and confidence-building strategies for successful interviews in Japanese settings.

Study in Japan
Complete guidance for pursuing higher education in Japan with comprehensive support from application to arrival.
What's Included
Japanese Language Proficiency: To offer high-quality Japanese language instruction tailored to the needs of learners at various proficiency levels, enabling them to communicate effectively in both everyday situations and professional contexts.
Japanese Language Exam Preparation: To assist students in preparing for standardized Japanese language proficiency exams such as JLPT, NAT-TEST, J-TEST, JLCT, J-Cert, Top-J, Skill Test-JFT Basic etc. equipping them with the skills and strategies needed to succeed.
School & College Interview Preparation: To provide comprehensive guidance and practice sessions to prepare students for admissions interviews in Japan, focusing on effective communication, cultural awareness and professionalism.
Documentation Assistance Services: Customized support services for document preparation, review, translation and submission tailored to meet the specific requirements of visa applications and academic admissions applications in Japan.
Translation Services: To provide accurate and reliable translation services for documents, certificates, resumes and other materials from English and Nepalese language to Japanese and vice versa, ensuring clarity and precision in communication.
Pre-departure Orientation Program to Japan Guidance: Informative sessions covering essential topics such as airport guidance, airport transit, documents needed in Immigration office at Japan airport, cultural adaptation, accommodation options, healthcare, banking, transportation and safety tips for living and studying in Japan.
Career Counselling Sessions: Individualized career counselling sessions offering personalized advice, goal-setting assistance, industry insights, resume/CV reviews and networking opportunities to help students navigate their career pathways in Japan effectively.
Post-arrival Support Services: Update about student’s activities and study by coordinating with the school & college. We are always there to help if needed for the students related to college transfer, part time job.

Our Process from Apply to Japan Arrival
1.	Initial Counseling 
2.	Academic Documents Verification
3.	Financial Documents Briefing
4.	Meeting with the parents (If Needed)
5.	Admission to Basic Japanese Language Class
6.	Discussion about City choice/Selection of City and School
7.	School/College Interview Preparation Class
8.	School/College Interview then Selection and Application
9.	Application Form fill up, Document Preparation and Submission to School/College
10.	Certificate of Eligibility (COE) processing
11.	Certificate of Eligibility (COE) granted by Japan Immigration Office
12.	NOC Process 
13.	Japan College Fee Payment by “A” Class Bank
14.	VFX Date Book
15.	Embassy Form fill up and Apply
16.	Embassy Interview preparation
17.	Visa Receive
18.	Ticketing Guideline
19.	Pre-departure orientation
20.	Arrival support and follow-up
